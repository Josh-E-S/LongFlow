   **[RETRACTED hours later, 2026-08-11, by Gate Night 1 cell 5.]** heun8 is not
   shippable: WER **0.238** vs euler4's 0.079, worse in 5/6 utterances, with
   semantic corruption. euler4 is intelligible but under-dispersed (the E1/E1b
   fade). **No sampler is both — the headline-sampler question is OPEN and the
   fix is the head, not the integrator.** Surviving rule: always name the
   sampler next to the number. Upgraded conclusion: **P2 MeanFlow is
   load-bearing for correctness, not just speed.** Full A/B in
   `experiments/p1_flow_head/NOTES.md` (Gate Night 1 entry). RTF levers: CFG-stream
   removal, P4 encoder distillation, batching (5×, built). **P2 MeanFlow is
   now load-bearing** — the only route back to a large head-level number at
   correct dispersion.
2. **Windowed context promoted from side-arm toward core.** E3 established the
   dominant failure axis is **context-length OOD** (long text prefix → hidden
   states off-distribution from frame zero). No finite cache of short/medium
   utterances makes 90-minute conditions in-distribution — the sink+window
   mechanism is the only structural fix in the plan, it bounds attention cost,
   and it keeps conditioning stats inside the trained regime. C3 embedding-
   blend anchoring demoted to ablation arm.
   **[2026-08-11, Gate Night 2]** Promotion stands, on narrower grounds:
   chunked text at practical sizes does **not** cure N8's rate inflation
   (237 wpm vs 165 natural), so windowing's justification is drift mitigation
   + bounded conditioning stats — not pacing. Pacing fix = open question
   (NOTES, GN2 verdict).
3. **Data mix bias toward long context.** The 30/30/25/15 frames rebudget is
   directionally right; amendments: source dialogue/long scripts from real
   podcast transcripts (not purely LLM-generated — narrow prosodic/discourse
   distribution); stratify held-out sets by **context length as well as
   speaker** (current eval fixes the speaker axis only — a head that dies at
   long prefixes would pass it).
   **[qualified 2026-08-11 by Gate Night 1 cell 7 → finding N8]** Long context
   remains the right axis, but long-form *teacher captures are not clean
   prosody targets*: VibeVoice inflates speaking rate **1.5×** (252 vs 168 wpm)
   on a 3229-word script, from the first 30 s. Training on them distills the
   defect. Before the mix is rebudgeted, decide: rate-correct the captures,
   source long context another way, or accept and document inherited pacing.
4. **Capacity probe scope.** Conditioning is now proven good (P1 gate), so
   constraint 5's original rationale (don't paper over conditioning bugs with
   capacity) no longer bars a **shallow local-attention variant (2–4 frames)**
   in the queued capacity probe alongside width-960. The residual "digital
   cold" texture may be a one-frame-conditioning limit; cheap to test on the
   existing cache.

## 4. Recipe alignment — the video world already drew our map

First-principles check "is there a method outside speech nobody has used?"
answered YES — and it is the plan we already derived independently. The
2025–26 autoregressive **video** distillation literature converged on a
standard recipe for exactly our problem shape (slow diffusion model, long
autoregressive rollout, drift):

| Our phase / idea | Video-world counterpart | Refs |
|---|---|---|
| P1: offline head distillation on teacher's cached states (teacher-forcing) | CausVid-style causal distillation; TMD flow-head grafting on frozen backbone | Yin et al. 2025 (CausVid); TMD 2601.09881 |
| Queued "DAgger-style on-policy distillation" (NOTES, Integration findings) | **Self Forcing** — train the student on its own rollout histories to kill exposure bias | Huang et al. 2025; Self-Forcing++ (Cui 2025) |
| Combined TF→SF pipeline | **Causal-rCM** unified teacher-forcing→self-forcing open recipe; Causal Forcing (ICML 2026) for proper init | 2606.25473; thu-ml/Causal-Forcing |
| P3 windowed/anchored context (attention sink keeps voice prompt in view + sliding window) | **DySink** (dynamic frame sinks for long AR video), TetherCache (gated recall) | 2605.21028; 2606.13035 |
| C4 drift curves (quality vs. position) | Long-video extrapolation drift evals | SGF 2607.20368 and cites therein |

**Nobody has instantiated this recipe in speech** (speech is doing offline
MeanFlow distillation only, as of today's search). Consequences:

- Import battle-tested recipes with published failure modes instead of
  inventing stages 2–3; cite the lineage explicitly.
- Paper framing upgrades to: *first application of the causal-distillation
  recipe (TF head distillation → on-policy self-forcing → sink/window context)
  to long-form multi-speaker TTS, on a frozen third-party system* — plus the
  conditioning-provenance finding the video papers have no analog of.
- Confirms amendment 2: on-policy stage + windowed context are the recipe's
  second and third acts, not polish — video results say drift does not fully
  die without them.
- What does NOT change: the frozen backbone (our constraint AND our novelty)
  means we cannot distill the whole model like video does → e2e speed stays
  Amdahl-capped → the paper's value stays science + stability + first-packet
  latency.
